V10 Momentum Engine — costed, position-sized backtest

4,279 of 4,280 ledger signals replayed on hourly Binance USDT-M data, 2021-09-06 → 2026-09-25. One account, $100k start, open positions marked daily and liquidated at the last bar. Paper research, not advice.

Bottom line: Realistic costs cut the ledger's result by about a quarter. Sizing it as one real account takes it further down, to +440% over five years (≈40% a year) with a −77% drawdown. All of the profit comes from a handful of runners, and most of those are still open and marked at today's price. Without the 5 best trades, every scenario loses money. On engines, the data does not support dropping IGNITION or PHOENIX_IGNITION. It does support dropping PHOENIX_CONTINUATION.

What this shows

  • 1 · Costs. Next-bar fills, 0.05% fees, square-root slippage and funding take the same trades and sizing from +805% to +596%. Sharpe falls from 1.02 to 0.92. Funding paid is the largest cost ($74k), then slippage ($34k) and fees ($7k). Next-bar timing on its own changes little, with one exception: toxic_trail exits are booked at the trailing level (peak × 0.75) even when the hourly close is far below it. Their average falls from +59.6% in the ledger to +32.4% when filled at the next open. That is a bookkeeping bug in the engine.
  • 2 · Sizing and a real equity curve. Risking 0.5% of equity per trade, with caps (5% per position, 2× gross, 40 positions, 0.5% of 24h volume, winners trimmed at 20%), gives ≈40% a year. But it comes with a −77% drawdown (Aug 2025) and uneven years: 2022 −17%, 2023 +158%, 2024 −37%, 2025 −8%, 2026 +339%. Starting the same rules at any quarter still gives drawdowns of −70% to −83%.
  • Concentration. In scenario C the positions still open at the end are worth +$620k, more than the whole +$440k gain, so the trades already closed lost money in aggregate. The profit factor is only 1.48 once open runners are counted. After costs the median trade is negative for every engine (−3% to −16%).
  • Capacity. The edge shrinks with size: $1m of starting equity earns ≈25% a year against ≈40% at $100k, because liquidity caps and impact bind on the small coins where the big moves happen. Cutting risk to 0.25% did better than 0.5% (≈50% a year, −68% drawdown). Raising it to 1% gave about the same return with a deeper −78% drawdown.
  • 3 · Engines. Per trade after costs, IGNITION (−3.3%), PHOENIX_CONTINUATION (−4.0%) and PHOENIX_IGNITION (−3.3%) do lose on average. But at the portfolio level, IGNITION (+379%, Sharpe 1.01) and PHOENIX_IGNITION (+588%, Sharpe 1.22) are the two best engines on their own. Their few 10×–100× runners outweigh the many small stop-outs. Keeping only CONTINUATION + squeezes (E) falls to ≈12% a year.
  • Walk-forward check. Choosing engines on the first half of the data (which drops IGNITION) and trading the second half returned +81%, against +351% for all engines. The same trimming would have hurt. PHOENIX_CONTINUATION is the only engine that is negative in every view: per trade, out of sample (−4.4%) and on its own (−53%). Removing it (D) lifts CAGR from 40% to 45% and improves the drawdown from −77% to −69%.
  • Funding data caveat. The stored funding series is a forward-filled prevailing rate, not settlement records. Two coins (MYX, LAB) would each have received more than 17× their position size in funding, which is not believable. So funding received is excluded by default. If it were counted, C would rise to +882%; assuming a 4h settlement interval, it would drop to +264%.

Assumptions

  • Fills: the engine decides on a closed hourly bar, so entries and exits fill at the next bar's open. The ledger books the signal bar's close.
  • Fees: 0.05% per side (Binance USDT-M VIP0 taker).
  • Slippage: half-spread 0.05% + Y·σdaily·√(order / 24h volume), with Y = 1.0. Volume and volatility are trailing values known at decision time.
  • Funding: longs pay the prevailing rate, accrued hourly as rate ÷ 8h and scaled by price drift. Funding received (negative rates) is not counted by default. The stored series is a forward-filled prevailing rate, not settlement records, and a few coins show implausible credits: MYX -1,893% of notional, LAB -1,743% of notional. Net funding and a 4h interval are shown under Sensitivity.
  • Sizing: risk 0.50% of equity to the initial stop. Caps: 5% of equity per position, 0.5% of 24h volume, 2× gross exposure, 40 positions. A signal that doesn't fit is skipped, not queued. A winner is trimmed back to 20% of equity at the daily mark once it passes 1.25× that (fees and impact charged). Without trimming, one or two runners end up as most of the account.
  • Initial stops follow the engine code: entry-candle low (CONTINUATION, PHOENIX_*), −20% (squeeze engines), and the lowest low of the 12-bar arming window for IGNITION (never tighter than the real one). Minimum stop distance for sizing is 2%.
  • Not modelled: exits inside the bar (a stop triggers only on an hourly close, as in the engine), delisting gaps, liquidation (gross ≤ 2×, and positions never go below 0), and survivorship in the symbol list. Not replayable: no price shard (1).

Recommended changes

  • Switch off PHOENIX_CONTINUATION, or require it to pass a new out-of-sample test before re-enabling. Keep IGNITION and PHOENIX_IGNITION; their negative average trade is the cost of the rare runner.
  • Fix the toxic_trail exit so it books the actual close (or next open), not peak × 0.75. As it stands, ledger P&L for those exits is overstated by about 27 points on average.
  • Store funding as settlement events (fundingTime + rate + interval from fundingInfo) instead of a forward-filled hourly rate, so funding can be measured rather than estimated.
  • If this is ever run with money: size at 0.25–0.5% risk to the stop, trim winners at around 20% of equity, keep gross exposure at 2× or less, and treat roughly $100k–$1m as the capacity range. Expect multi-year flat or negative stretches and drawdowns near −70%. This is research, not advice.
  • Next research step: the ledger holds 177 positions older than 30 days. Test a time stop or a Donchian ratchet for IGNITION/PHOENIX_IGNITION that keeps the runners but frees capital from dormant losers.
